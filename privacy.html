Privacy Policy

App Name: Ensō No Distraction Watch Face

Effective Date: March 31, 2026

1. Developer Data Practices

The developer of the Ensō No Distraction Watch Face does not collect, store, or retain any personal information, health data, or usage statistics. No data is ever transmitted to any external servers managed by the developer.

The watch face functions as a local interface only. All health data (Heart Rate and Steps) is read directly from the device's sensors and displayed on the screen in real-time. This information is not recorded or archived by the watch face app itself.

2. Permissions

To provide its core functionality, this watch face requests the following permissions:

  • Body Sensors (Heart Rate): Used only to display your current BPM on the watch face.
  • Physical Activity (Steps): Used only to display your daily step count.

These permissions are used solely for real-time visual display on the device.

3. Third-Party Platform Data

This watch face runs on the Wear OS platform and may be installed on devices manufactured by third parties (such as Samsung, Google, etc.).

Platform Processing: Your watch hardware and operating system may collect, sync, and process health and usage data as part of their core system functions (e.g., syncing with Samsung Health or Google Fit). This is independent of the watch face.

Governing Policies: Any such data collection is managed entirely by the platform provider and/or device manufacturer. This data is subject to the respective Privacy Policies and Terms of Service of Google and the Device Manufacturer (e.g. Google, Samsung, etc.). The developer of this watch face has no access to, or control over, this platform-level data collection.
